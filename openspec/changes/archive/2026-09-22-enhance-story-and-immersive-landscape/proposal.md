# Proposal

## Why

Keyword-only dialogue routes feel repetitive, and exterior subtitles separate narration from the scene. This change makes story progression relationship-aware and presents dialogue in an immersive, landscape-capable scene.

## What Changes

- Add relationship, clue, and story-beat driven dialogue selection.
- Add scene-integrated narrator and Mira subtitle treatment.
- Add user-initiated immersive landscape entry with a safe portrait fallback.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `immersive-story-presentation`: Natural story progression, diegetic subtitles, and landscape scene-entry behavior.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Scene renderer, session response contract, mobile orientation handling, and Mock story fixtures.
